No, MetoHub does not currently support built-in online payment gateways (like UPI, credit/debit cards, or net banking) for customer checkouts. According to the MetoHub FAQ, customer checkout only supports Cash on Delivery (COD), and marking an order as "paid" in your dashboard is strictly for manual record-keeping rather than processing or transferring money.

How Payments Work on MetoHub

• Customer Checkout: Shoppers place orders using Cash on Delivery without paying online at checkout.
• Manual Payment Tracking: If a customer pays you separately via GPay, PhonePe, or a direct bank transfer, you can manually mark the order as "paid" in the dashboard to keep your records organized.
• Platform Billing Only: MetoHub uses Razorpay solely to collect your monthly or annual subscription fee—not to process payments from your customers.
• Zero Commission: Because it doesn't route online transactions through a payment gateway, MetoHub charges 0% commission on your sales.

What to Do If You Need Online Payments

• Workaround on MetoHub: You can still use MetoHub as a clean catalog and order-taking system, then send customers your personal UPI ID or payment QR code via WhatsApp once an order comes in.
• Alternative Platforms: If you want customers to pay directly at checkout via UPI, cards, or wallets like a standard e-commerce site, you will need a platform with native payment gateway integrations (such as Shopify, Dukaan, Bikayi, or WooCommerce).
